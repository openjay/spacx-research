# Terminology and translation guardrails

SPACX is bilingual, but English securities terms must not be translated mechanically.
Use this glossary for public docs, workstreams, packets, and agent-generated drafts.

## Securities terms

| English source term | Approved Chinese rendering | Avoid | Notes |
|---------------------|----------------------------|-------|-------|
| `over-allotment option` / `greenshoe` | **超额配售选择权（greenshoe）** | 绿鞋、绿鞋股、绿靴 | Keep `greenshoe` in parentheses for traceability to S-1/A language. |
| `free writing prospectus` / `FWP` | **自由书面招股说明书（FWP）** or **FWP 发行沟通材料** | 最终招股书、定价招股书 | FWP is Rule 433 offering communication; it does not clear 424B4 blockers. |
| `final prospectus` / `424B4` | **最终招股说明书（Form 424B4）** | 上市确认、交易确认 | 424B4 confirms final offering terms; first trade is a separate confirmation. |
| `underwriting discount / commission` | **承销折扣/佣金** | 承销费用（when referring to the blank pricing-table spread） | Offering expenses and underwriting spread are distinct. |
| `offering expenses` | **发行费用** | 承销折扣 | Keep separate from underwriting discounts/commissions. |
| `proposed / expected listing symbol` | **拟上市/预期上市代码** | 已上市代码、上市股票 | Until 424B4 and first trade are confirmed. |

## Agent rule

If an AI-generated Chinese draft introduces a literal translation not listed above,
prefer the English term plus a precise Chinese legal/market description. Add new
terms here before publishing repeated bilingual usage.
