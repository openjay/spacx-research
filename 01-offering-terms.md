## Use of proceeds (summary)

SEC exact quote — net proceeds for: **expansion of our AI compute infrastructure**; **enhancements to our launch infrastructure and launch vehicles**; **increases in the scale and capacity of our satellite constellations**; and **any remaining amounts for general corporate purposes** (discretionary residual). (*Use of Proceeds*, p. 67)

## Lock-up (summary)

- **180-day** standard lock-up with staged early releases (earnings-linked; +10% tranche if stock ≥30% above $135 for 5/10 days around first earnings).
- **Extended** lock-up for certain holders through **Q2 2027** earnings (excludes Musk).
- **Musk: 366 days**, **no early release** (~6.4B shares on day 366).
- **>1-year cohort:** ~7.8B shares — **~60% post-IPO** outstanding (*Shares Eligible*); **>63% pre-IPO** (*Underwriting*). See `audit/B-lock-up-schedule.md`.
