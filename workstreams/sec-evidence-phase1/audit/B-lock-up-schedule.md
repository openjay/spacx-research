## Audit flags (lock-up)

1. **Denominator inconsistency (not a math error):** *Shares Eligible* states **~60%** of shares outstanding **after** offering; *Underwriting* states **>63%** **immediately prior** to offering — different bases.
2. **SEC typo:** Extended lock-up table contains duplicated phrase “**of the of the**” before “shares subject to the extended lock-up period” (cosmetic in filing).
3. **Cumulative % logic:** 180-day schedule uses **cumulative** “up to X%” language in narrative; dollar table gives **max shares per tranche** — model supply as path-dependent (earnings dates unknown until priced).
4. **Directed shares:** **Five percent** reserved; **lock-up exempt** only if actually purchased in that program.
